### OctoMap

- Enhance voxels with occupancy likelihood

  $$
  o_{ijk} =
  \begin{cases}
  1 & \text{if voxel is occupied} \\
  0 & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
  $$

- $p(o_{ijk} \mid Z_{1:t}) \to$ Probability of occupancy of voxel $ijk$, given the measurements till current time

- Log odds

  $$
  \mathcal{L}(o_{ijk} \mid Z_{1:t}) = \frac{p(o_{ijk} \mid Z_{1:t})}{1 - p(o_{ijk} \mid Z_{1:t})}
  $$

$$
p(o) + p(\neg o) = 1
$$

### Signed distance function

$$
\operatorname{SDF}(P) = \text{Distance of } P \text{ to the nearest surface}
$$

$$
\operatorname{SDF}_{\text{circle}}(x, y) = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} - r
$$

- Implicit representation of shapes
- Truncated signed distance function ($\operatorname{TSDF}$)

- Motion planning with $\operatorname{ESDF}$ (Euclidean $\operatorname{SDF}$)

  - Collision costs

    $$
    J_c = \int_S c(\mathbf{f}(t)) \, ds
    , \qquad
    c(\mathbf{x}) =
    \begin{cases}
    - d(\mathbf{x}) + \frac{1}{2} \epsilon & \text{if } d(\mathbf{x}) < 0 \\
    \frac{1}{2 \epsilon} {(d(\mathbf{x}) - \epsilon)}^2 & \text{if } 0 \leq d(\mathbf{x}) \leq \epsilon \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
    $$

### Voxblox

![Voxblox](./Images/2025-04-04*/1.png)

- Updating $\operatorname{TSDF}$

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}, \mathbf{s}) &= \|\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{x}\| \, \text{sign}\left((\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{x}) \cdot (\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{s})\right) \\
  w_{\text{const}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) &= 1 \\
  D_{i+1}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) &= \frac{W_i(\mathbf{x}) D_i(\mathbf{x}) + w(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p})}{W_i(\mathbf{x}) + w(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p})} \\
  W_{i+1}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) &= \min\left(W_i(\mathbf{x}) + w(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}), W_{\max}\right)
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  $$
  w_{\text{quad}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) =
  \begin{cases}
  \frac{1}{z^2} & -\epsilon < d \\
  \frac{1}{z^2} \cdot \frac{1}{\delta - \epsilon}(d + \delta) & -\delta < d < -\epsilon \\
  0 & d < -\delta
  \end{cases}
  $$

  - Truncation distance $\delta = 4v$, $\epsilon = v$
  - $v \to$ voxel size

- Fusing multiple $\operatorname{TSDF}$

- Clipping of weights allows capturing dynamic scenes

- Earlier works used constant weight $\implies$ simple averaging

## NERF SLAM

- Perceptron

  - Basic element of a Neural network (NN)

  $$
  s_i = \mathbf{w}_i^\top \mathbf{x}_i + b_i, \quad y_i = h(s_i)
  $$

  - $\mathbf{w}_i, \mathbf{x}_i \to$ Parameters that need to learnt
  - $h \to$ Activation function
    - Needs to be non-linear

- Multi-layer perceptrons (MLP)

  - MLPs form a Neural network
    $$
    \widehat{\mathbf{w}} = \arg \min_{\mathbf{w}} \sum_{i} \mathcal{L}_i (\operatorname{NN}(D_i, \mathbf{w}))
    $$

  - Training data $D = \{ D_i \}$

- A Neural network is a universal function approximator
  - Any continuous function can be approximated as closely as needed with enough layers and units in each layer

- Backpropagation

  - To calculate gradients

- SLAM components

- Novel view synthesis

- Neural radiance field $F_{\Theta}: \mathbb{R}^3 \times \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2 \times \mathbb{R}$

- Stratified sampling for rendering the scene

- Positional encodings

- Coarse + Fine sampling

  - $N_c$ coarse samples

    $$
    \hat{C}_c(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{i = 1}^{N_c} w_i c_i, \quad w_i = T_i(1 - \exp(-\sigma_i \delta_i))
    $$

    $$
    \hat w_i = \frac{w_i}{\sum_{j = 1}^{N_c} w_j}
    $$

  - Photometric loss

    $$
    \mathcal{L} = \sum_{\mathbf{r} \in \mathcal{R}} \bigg[ {\Big\Vert \hat{C}_c(\mathbf{r}) - C(\mathbf{r}) \Big\Vert}^2_2 + {\Big\Vert \hat{C}_f(\mathbf{r}) - C(\mathbf{r}) \Big\Vert}^2_2 \bigg]
    $$
