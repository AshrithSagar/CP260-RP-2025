## Review

- $H_{3 \times 4}$

  - 3D homography matrix

  - Has rank $3$

  - Can be broken down into

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    H & = K \ {}^cM_w \ P_w \\
    K & = \underbrace{ \begin{bmatrix} f_x & s & c_{0x} \\ 0 & f_y & c_{0y} \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} }_{\text{intrinsic}} \\
    {}^cM_w & = \begin{bmatrix} R_{3 \times 3} & t_{3 \times 1} \end{bmatrix}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

### Planar homography

- $H_{3 \times 3}: \mathbb{P}^2 \to \mathbb{P}^2$
- $\mathbb{P}^2 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{R}^2 \cup \{ \text{Point at infinity} \}$

- $\left\{ \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{bmatrix}, z \neq 0 \right\}$
- $z \neq 0$ for a finite point

- In a homography, collinearity is preserved

- Group of points form a line, and group of lines form a point

  $$
  l = \begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad p = \begin{bmatrix} p_x \\ p_y \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
  $$

  $$
  l^\top p = 0
  $$

  $$
  p \xrightarrow{H} p' \implies p' = H p
  $$

  $$
  \implies l^\top p = l^\top H^{-1} H p = {(H^{-\top} l)}^\top (H p) = 0 \implies l'^\top p' = 0
  $$

  $$
  l' = {(H^{-\top} l)}^\top
  $$

### Euclidean

$$
H^E_{3 \times 3} = \begin{bmatrix} R & t \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

- Isometric

- Similarly
  $$
  H^S_{3 \times 3} = \begin{bmatrix} SR & t \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
  $$

### Affine homography

- 6 DOF

$$
H^A_{3 \times 3} = \begin{bmatrix} A & t \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

- Parallel lines preserved

### Projective homography

- $H_{3 \times 3}$

- In general, 8 parameters
- Invariances
  - Collinearity, cross ratios

- Homogeneous coordinates

- All points at infinity, lie on a line at infinity
  $$
  l = H^{-\top} l_{\infty}
  $$

  - The incremental transformation
    $$
    {H_U}_{3 \times 3} = \begin{bmatrix} I_{2 \times 2} \mid 0 \\ l^\top \end{bmatrix}
    $$

$$
H = \begin{bmatrix} A & t \\ d^\top & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} I & 0 \\ d^\top A^{-1} & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} A & t \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\text{Taking }
l_\infty = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
\text{ gives }
H^{-\top} l_{\infty} = \begin{bmatrix} -A^{-\top} d \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
H^{-1} = {(H^A)}^{-1} {(H_U)}^{-1} \implies H^{-\top} = {(H_U)}^{-\top} {(H^A)}^{-\top}
$$

- ~In an affine transformation, parallel lines still map to parallel lines
- Undoing transformation

## Conics

- Cicle, Ellipse, Parabola, Hyperbola

- Equation of a conic
  $$
  a x_1^2 + b x_1 x_2 + c x_2^2 + d x_1 x_3 + e x_2 x_3 + f x_3^2 = 0
  $$

- In projective plance, can be written as $\{ p |\mid p^\top C p \}$, where
  $$
  C = \begin{bmatrix} a & b/2 & d/2 \\ b/2 & c & e/2 \\ d/2 & e/2 & f \end{bmatrix}
  $$

  - Homogeneous representation of conic
  - 6 parameters, 5 free parameters
  - Is symmetric

- 5 points will define a point (conic) (~unique)
- Similarly, 5 lines also define a conic
